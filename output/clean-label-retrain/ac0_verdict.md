# ac-0 — Label-trust audit verdict (clean-label retrain)

_Author's non-negotiable: sample-check every label source before training, or we just
swap distilled-noise for vocab-noise._ Full per-family samples: `ac0_label_trust_audit.md`.
Generated by `scripts/audit_clean_labels.py`.

## Headline

**Vocab-membership is a trustworthy *generator of clean positives*, but a noisy *filter
on real columns*.** So the clean-label blend ADDS generator positives (clean by
construction) and REPLACES — never relabels-by-filtering — the noisy real rows. This is
exactly the trap the author flagged: filtering real columns by membership would inject new
false-drop noise.

## Per-family findings (real v3 rows, membership keep-rate eyeballed)

| family | v3 pass-rate | filter verdict | what the DROPs are |
|---|---|---|---|
| city | 60% | **noisy filter** | false-drops "City, ST" formats, sub-15k-pop towns, diacritics (München) — many DROPs are *real* cities |
| country | 84% | mixed | correctly drops band/company noise (Exitmusic, "Tsuru Capital LLC") BUT false-drops short forms ("United States" ≠ ISO "United States of America") |
| country_code | 78% | **contaminated** | US state abbreviations (CA, CO, IN, IL) pass as country codes — membership can't separate state_code |
| region | 81% (admin1-only **21%**) | **fix works** | the admin1 collapse is real and severe; the improved matcher (admin1+admin2+suffix+states+ISO) recovers it; DROPs are genuine noise (TeX fonts, "Youth Only") |
| continent | 87% | clean | small closed set; DROPs are UN macro-regions, correctly excluded |
| full_name | 63% (token) | **too strict** | false-drops initials-heavy and non-Anglo real names — token lists under-cover |

## Generator clean positives — TRUSTED (sampled, all families)

GeoNames geo (city/country/country_code/region/continent) and Wikidata person (full_name)
generator output is genuinely clean and well-formed: real GeoNames names across 15 locales,
full ISO country/code lists, improved-region names (départements, Bundesländer,
voivodeships), structurally-valid full names. No false-drop risk because they are generated
FROM the vocab, not filtered against it.

**Known caveat (watch at scoring):** generator columns under-cover the real "City, ST"
city format — if clean city regresses on gold specifically, this is the diagnosis.
Generator full names are cross-cultural given×family combinations (structurally valid,
semantically artificial); acceptable for "this column is a full_name" structure learning.

## Decision → the blend (`build_clean_label_blend.py`)

REPLACE the six target families' noisy v3 rows with clean generator positives at the
shipped caps (geography 3000, identity 600). Keep all non-target families untouched.
`label_remap.json` folds the generator's `us_state`/`county` → `region` and
`country_code_iso3` → `country_code`, so region — the former collapse risk — gets the
richest clean coverage. The structural geo (postal/lat/lon) is excluded: Sharpen owns it.
